FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I use CDM outside Nigeria?

Yes. CDM ERP, CDM School, CDM FinRep and CDM Prep work anywhere, and customers abroad can pay in US dollars, pounds or euros. CDM People and CDM Clearance follow Nigerian payroll and tax rules, so they are for organisations in Nigeria.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Most products can also be added to your home screen like an app.

Is there a free trial?

Our business products come with a 14-day free trial, and CDM Prep has a free account so you can start practising straight away.

How is my data protected?

Every organisation's records are kept separate and only people you give access to can see them. Connections are encrypted and the databases are backed up regularly.

Can I get a demo or help choosing?

Yes. Call, WhatsApp or email us and we will show you around and recommend the right plan.
